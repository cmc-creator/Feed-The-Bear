About

      
NyxSurvive is an offline-first tactical survival and bug-out management system: gear loadouts with honest weights, an AES-encrypted vault for plans and rally points, rotation tracking for the stores that quietly expire, and a comms plan that still works on paper. V1 runs entirely on-device - no account, no cloud, no signal required.
