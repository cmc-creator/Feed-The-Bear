Feed The Bear

Delete your account

Feed The Bear is made by NyxCollective LLC. You can delete your account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Feed The Bear and sign in.
  2. Tap your bear avatar in the top corner to open your profile.
  3. Choose Delete Account, type DELETE, and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away. You can open the app here.

By email

If you can't get into the app, email support@feed-the-bear.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account.
  • Everything backed up with it: spots, ratings, notes, dishes, photos, videos, lists and profile.
  • Anything you shared with friends through your profile link.
  • Any Grizzly subscription, which is cancelled so you're not charged again.

What we keep

Anonymous daily usage totals (like "spots added today"), which contain no user ID and can't be linked to you. Payment records Stripe must keep by law stay with Stripe. Nothing else is kept.

Data saved only on your device stays there unless you choose "Also erase everything saved on this device" when deleting, or clear the app's data.

See also our Privacy Policy.
